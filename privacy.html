Privacy Policy: Nusta Curiosity publishing tool

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The "nusta-curiosity" app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Nusta Curiosity YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule the channel's own videos, set thumbnails and captions, manage the channel's own video details and comments, and read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports.

What it stores

A sign-in token and the channel's own video and analytics data are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact us through the Nusta Curiosity YouTube channel.
